CCTV Maintenance & Troubleshooting

CCTV · 9 min read

What you'll learn

  • Routine CCTV maintenance tasks that prevent failures
  • Common camera and NVR issues and their usual causes
  • Quick checks to isolate whether the fault is cable, power, or network
  • How to keep remote viewing reliable over time

CCTV systems are reliable until they are not. The difference between a minor annoyance and a security incident is usually a simple maintenance habit. This checklist is based on deployments I support in municipal and private environments.

1. Routine maintenance

  • Clean camera lenses and domes — dust, rain and insects degrade image quality fast
  • Check cable glands and waterproofing on outdoor units
  • Inspect PoE injectors and switch ports for heat or loose connections
  • Verify NVR disk health and free space weekly

2. Common camera issues

When a camera goes dark or the image degrades, isolate the cause quickly:

  • No image: check PoE, cable continuity, and port link status
  • Pixelated or washed out: clean lens, check exposure settings, verify IR cut filter behaviour
  • Intermittent dropouts: look for a flaky Ethernet cable, duplex mismatch, or a failing PoE port
  • Wrong colours: white balance or lighting change; re-balance during daytime

3. NVR and storage checks

  • Confirm recording schedule is still enabled after firmware updates
  • Check disk SMART status and replace before failure
  • Review retention policy — if disks fill too fast, lower bitrate or add storage
  • Test playback on at least one camera weekly

4. Remote access

Remote viewing failures are usually port forwarding or DDNS issues. Verify:

  • Public IP and port mapping are still correct
  • DDNS hostname resolves to the current IP
  • App or client software is updated
  • Two-factor or VPN access is preferred over open ports where possible
A CCTV system you cannot trust during an incident is not a security system — it is a recording habit. Maintenance is what turns recording into reliability.

5. Quick maintenance schedule

  • Weekly: NVR disk and recording check
  • Monthly: camera cleaning, cable and PoE inspection
  • Quarterly: firmware review, remote access test, playback spot-check
